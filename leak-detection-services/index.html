Warning Signs

Signs You May Have a Hidden Water Leak

Most water leaks start out of sight — under the slab, inside a wall, or along the line to the street. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a leak detection plumber.

A Water Bill That Keeps Climbing

Your usage hasn't changed, but the bill has. A hidden leak can run 24 hours a day without you ever seeing it.

Warm or Damp Spots on the Floor

A warm patch on tile or wet carpet with no obvious source is a classic sign of a slab leak in a hot water line.

Running Water When Everything's Off

Hearing water moving through the pipes — or watching the meter spin — when no fixtures are on.

Stains, Mold or Musty Smells

Water stains on walls or ceilings, bubbling paint, or a musty odor point to a pipe leak behind the drywall.

Sudden Drop in Water Pressure

Weak flow at multiple fixtures can mean water is escaping somewhere along the supply line.

Cracks in the Slab or Foundation

New cracks in flooring or the foundation, or soggy soil around the house, can be caused by a leak underneath.

Slab Leak Detection & Repair

We Find Slab Leaks Without Tearing Up Your Floors

A slab leak is a break in a water line running beneath your home's concrete foundation. Left alone, it wastes water, drives up your bill, and can damage flooring and the foundation itself.

We use electronic leak detection equipment to pinpoint the exact location before any concrete is touched — so we open only what's needed, not your whole floor. Then we lay out your repair options in plain language with upfront pricing.

  • Precise electronic slab leak detection
  • Spot repair, line reroute, or repipe — your choice
  • Minimal digging and a clean job site
  • Recurring slab leaks? We'll tell you if the whole line is failing
