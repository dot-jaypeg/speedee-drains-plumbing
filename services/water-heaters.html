Tank vs. Tankless

Which Water Heater Is Right for Your Home?

Both tank and tankless units are solid, proven options. We'll walk you through the trade-offs so you can choose what fits your household and your budget.

Tank Water Heaters

A traditional tank water heater stores and continuously heats a reserve of hot water. It typically has a lower upfront cost and a simpler installation, making it a straightforward, reliable choice for many households.

Tankless Water Heaters

A tankless unit heats water on demand instead of storing it, which means a continuous supply of hot water and a more compact footprint. Tankless systems typically have a longer lifespan than tank units, though they come with a higher upfront installation cost.
