What Is Hydro Jetting?

A True Full-Diameter Clean

A high-pressure stream of water, delivered through a specialized nozzle, blasts grease, mineral scale, sludge and root intrusion off the entire interior wall of the pipe — not just a narrow channel through the middle.

Cable Snake Alone

Punches a narrow channel straight through the clog. Grease and scale coating the pipe walls are often left behind, so the same clog tends to come right back.

Hydro Jetting

Scours the full 360° circumference of the pipe, stripping buildup off every wall and restoring flow much closer to the pipe's original capacity.

Why Jet Instead of Snake
Benefits

Built for Buildup a Snake Can't Reach

Jetting is especially effective on grease-heavy kitchen lines, older pipes with years of scale buildup, and lines with recurring root intrusion. It's also a smart preventative step for property owners who want to get ahead of clogs before they become emergencies.

  • Removes grease, scale and sludge a snake can't reach
  • Restores full pipe flow, not just a narrow channel
  • Effective preventative maintenance for recurring clogs
  • Safe for most common pipe materials when performed by trained techs
