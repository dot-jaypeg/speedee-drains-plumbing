How We Clear It
Our Process

Snaked, Inspected, and Verified Clean

Most clogs come down to grease, hair, soap buildup or roots working their way into the line. We start with mechanical snaking to clear the immediate blockage, then run a sewer camera to confirm the line is actually clear and check for any underlying issue causing the backup — like intruding roots or a damaged section of pipe.

If roots are found, we cut and clear them so water can flow freely again. And if the camera turns up something beyond a routine clog, we'll explain exactly what we're seeing and lay out your real options before any further work begins.

  • Sinks, tubs, showers, toilets and main line clogs
  • Sewer camera inspection to locate and verify the clog
  • Root removal when roots are the cause
  • Honest explanation of what the camera finds — no upselling
